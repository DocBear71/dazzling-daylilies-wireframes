Page Annotations: Admin Plant Editor — Revised (Wireframe 11)

Reference notes for WF11-REVISED. Decisions confirmed during wireframe review are noted below.

What Changed from Original Wireframe 11

The Coordinated With section now has two parts. The top part (chip selector for picking companion plants) is unchanged. A new "Collection Offer" subsection appears below it, separated by a horizontal rule. The section header and border are now green to signal it is updated. Everything else on the page is untouched.

Updated section
Valid Collection Size Indicator

Below the chip selector, a small line of text counts the currently selected plants and notes whether the count is a valid collection size (3, 5, 7, or 9). This helps Jean know at a glance whether her current selection is eligible for a collection offer. If she has 4 chips selected, the indicator would read "4 plants selected • Not a valid collection size (must be 3, 5, 7, or 9)" and the collection toggle would be disabled until she adds or removes a plant.

Collection Enable Toggle

Jean can have companion plants configured without offering a collection. If she has the chips set but the toggle is off, the editorial Coordinated Flowers grid still appears on the public plant detail page, but no collection panel is shown. The toggle is the switch between "these are editorial companions" and "these are a purchasable collection." Jean can turn the collection on or off at any time without losing the collection name, price, or description she has entered.

Collection Name Field

Optional. A human-friendly name for the collection set, like "Jean's Sunset Garden Collection." Shown on the collection detail page (Wireframe 03b) as the page title. If Jean leaves it blank, the system uses a default like "5-Plant Collection." The name adds the personal, curated quality that makes the collection feel like Jean's recommendation rather than an automated upsell.

Collection Price Field

Jean enters a single dollar amount for the entire collection. The system calculates and displays the individual total and the savings automatically in the live preview below. Jean does not need to calculate anything; she just decides what she wants to charge for the set as a whole.

Collection Description Field

Optional free-text field. Jean's own explanation of why these plants go together: bloom timing, color combinations, height, garden use. This appears at the top of the collection detail page (Wireframe 03b) and is the most important piece of copy for making the collection feel curated and trustworthy. Placeholder text in the field prompts Jean with an example of what to write.

Live Collection Price Preview

As Jean types a collection price, the preview box below the fields updates in real time to show: the individual total (sum of all plant prices in the set), the collection price she entered, and the customer savings in both dollars and percentage. This lets Jean see immediately whether her collection price makes sense before saving. If the collection price is higher than the individual total, the savings would show as negative and Jean would see a warning.

Database Changes Required

The plant_coordinations table needs three new columns to support collections: collection_enabled (boolean, default false), collection_price_cents (integer, nullable), collection_name (string, nullable), and collection_description (text, nullable). The collection_enabled flag is what controls whether the Collection panel shows on the public page. The price, name, and description are only used when a collection offer is active.
