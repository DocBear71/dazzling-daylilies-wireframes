Wireframe Notes: WF18 — Printable Inventory Report

Hi Jean! The notes below explain what each part of this page does and confirm the decisions we have already made together. When you have finished reading, please use the Approval Form at the bottom of this page to let Edward know whether the page looks right or whether you would like any changes.

How This Page Works

You access this report from the Admin area by clicking "Print Inventory Report." A new browser tab opens showing this page. Click the "Print Inventory Report" button at the top to open your browser's print dialog, then select your printer. Everything outside the white report area (the button, this header, and the notes below) disappears when printing so only the clean report goes to the printer. You can also use the browser's print-to-PDF option to save a copy.

Photos: Confirmed by Jean

Each plant shows a square photo above the plant's name and details, roughly 3 inches by 3 inches when printed. The report fits four plants across each row. Depending on your plant count per category, you will fit approximately six to eight plants per page including the category heading and summary line. This layout lets you flip through the list visually and identify plants at a glance.

Confirmed: Jean requested photos in her June 5, 2026 reply
Organization: By Category, Alphabetically Within Each

Plants are grouped under their category heading (Daylilies, Iris, Lilies, Dahlias, Japanese Iris, Other Perennials). Within each category, plants are sorted alphabetically by name. Only categories that are active in your settings appear on the report. At launch, that means Daylilies only; as you activate other tabs, those categories appear automatically.

Confirmed: category grouping and alphabetical sort
What Each Plant Card Shows

Each card shows: the plant photo, the plant name, abbreviated attributes (bloom size, ploidy, foliage type, rebloom, scape height), the price, and the current status (Active, Sold Out, or Held for Increase). Draft plants do not appear on this report since they are not yet ready for sale or display. The status label uses color coding so sold-out and held plants are easy to spot at a glance: green for active, red for sold out, yellow for held for increase.

Category Summary Line

Below each category's plant grid, a small summary line shows the total count for that category broken down by status. This gives you a quick count of how many are active, sold out, or held without having to count cards manually.

Report Footer Totals

At the very bottom of the report, a small totals block shows the grand total plant count across all categories, broken down by status. This lets you see your full inventory picture at a glance.

Paper Format and Print Settings

Designed for standard US letter paper (8.5 x 11 inches, portrait). In your browser's print dialog, set: Paper size: Letter, Orientation: Portrait, Margins: Default or Narrow. Uncheck "Print headers and footers" so the browser does not add its own URL and date lines to the printed pages. These instructions will be in your admin guide as a step-by-step reminder.

Multi-Page Reports

If you have many plants, the report will naturally span multiple pages. Category headings and plant cards are formatted to avoid awkward page breaks in the middle of a card. If a category does not fit on the remaining space of a page, it starts at the top of the next page.

At launch with Daylilies only, the report will likely be 3 to 5 pages depending on your plant count.
