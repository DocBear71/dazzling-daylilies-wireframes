About Jean and Dazzling Daylilies

Hi, I'm Jean, and I've been growing daylilies in Fairfax, Iowa for over twenty years. What started as a few plants along the fence has grown into a garden full of hundreds of varieties in every color imaginable. Every plant I sell has been grown right here in my own yard, and I dig and ship them fresh when the season is right.

I love connecting with other gardeners and helping people find the perfect plant for their space. If you have a question about a variety, a tricky spot in your yard, or you just want to talk daylilies, feel free to reach out. I'm always happy to chat.

Questions? Email me at dazzlingdaylilies@yahoo.com.

Wireframe Notes: WF01 — Homepage

Homepage (Wireframe 01)

Reference notes for WF01. Decisions confirmed during wireframe review are noted below.

Cookie Notice (dark strip at the very top)

Appears once when a customer first visits. It tells them the site only uses cookies to make the shopping cart work, nothing else. They click "Got it" and it disappears permanently. This is just basic legal housekeeping for a US-only site. No decision needed here.

Site Header: Business Name and Tagline

Your business name is the main heading. The tagline below is placeholder copy and needs your approval. This area will also hold your logo once it is finalized. The logo work we have been doing together will live here.

Content needed
Navigation: Two-Row Tab Bar

Because your contract includes six plant-type tabs (Daylilies, Iris, Lilies, Dahlias, Japanese Iris, and Other Perennials) plus a Collections tab, fitting everything in one row would cause the page to scroll sideways on smaller screens. To prevent that, the navigation uses two rows. Row 1 holds Daylilies, Iris, Lilies, and Collections. Row 2 holds Dahlias, Japanese Iris, and Other Perennials. At launch, only Daylilies is clickable. All others show "(coming soon)" until you activate them from your admin Settings page.

Per signed contract Section 3.1: six plant-type tabs
Hero Section (large banner photo with text overlay)

A big welcoming photo fills this area. A white text box sits over it with a short introduction and a button taking customers to the catalog. The white box keeps text readable regardless of what the photo looks like underneath.

Content needed
Featured Plants (4 cards)

Shows four hand-picked plants from your catalog. You control which ones appear here from the admin panel. On phones the cards stack vertically. One card shows how a "Sold Out" plant looks: gray badge, grayed price, disabled button reading "Currently Unavailable."

Decision needed
About Jean Section

Your photo on the left, a personal introduction on the right. This is one of the most important sections on the homepage because your customers are buying from you personally, not a faceless company. The text shown is placeholder copy.

Content needed
Footer: Three Columns

Left: contact info (email address shown; street address omitted unless you want it public). Middle: links to Shipping Information, Order Status, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service. Right: email newsletter signup.

Footer: Email Signup

Customers type their email and click Subscribe. The contract specifies a Mailchimp signup form. Email addresses collected here go into your Mailchimp list so you can send seasonal updates, new variety announcements, and shipping window reminders. Customers only receive emails if they sign up here; nothing is sent without their permission.

Page Width

Content is contained in a 1,160-pixel-wide column centered on screen, matching the width PlantFans uses. On wide monitors there will be some white space on the sides. On phones everything reflows to a single column with no sideways scrolling.
