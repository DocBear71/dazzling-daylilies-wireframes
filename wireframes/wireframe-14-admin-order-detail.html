Page Annotations: Admin Order Detail (Wireframe 14 of 17)

This is the page Jean works from when she processes, packs, and ships an order. The wireframe shows DD-2027-0036, a New order from a Texas address with the restricted-state flag active. Plain English throughout.

Previous / Next Order Navigation

Two small buttons at the top right let Jean step through orders without returning to the list. When she is working through a batch of new orders, this lets her open each one, process it, and move to the next without extra clicks. The buttons also appear at the bottom of the sidebar for convenience when she has scrolled down the page.

Restricted State Notice (amber banner)

This banner appears at the top of any order where the restricted_state_notice_shown flag is true. It reminds Jean that this customer is in a flagged state (TX, AZ, or FL), that they accepted the notice at checkout, and that Jean should verify the order can be shipped before packing. The customer's email address is linked directly so Jean can reach them with one click.

Confirmed: place order, hold for Jean to contact customer
Customer and Shipping Card

Shows the customer's full name, email address (linked as a mailto for one-click contact), phone number, and the complete shipping address. The two-column layout puts contact info on the left and shipping address on the right so both are visible at the same time. Jean uses the shipping address when printing the shipping label.

Plants Ordered Card

Full detail of every plant in the order: photo thumbnail, plant name, attributes, unit price, quantity, and line subtotal. The totals block shows subtotal, shipping, and total charged. These figures are snapshotted at the time of order and never change even if Jean later updates a plant's price. This is the same data that appears on the printed order sheet.

Internal Notes Card

A private text area for Jean to write notes about this specific order. Never shown to the customer. Pre-filled in the wireframe with example notes about the TX notice and a voicemail. Jean can add packing reminders, customer requests, or follow-up notes. The Save Note button saves without reloading the page. Notes persist and are visible every time Jean opens this order.

Order Status Card (sidebar): Mark as Processing and Mark as Shipped

Two status advancement buttons. "Mark as Processing" moves the order from New to Processing, the amber highlight disappears from the orders list, and no email is sent. "Mark as Shipped" records the shipped date, advances the status to Shipped, and again no automatic email is sent. The note below each button makes this explicit so Jean is not surprised. Jean emails the customer personally with tracking information, which is her stated preference.

Tracking Number Entry

A separate text input and Save button for entering the USPS tracking number. Once saved, the tracking number appears on the customer-facing Order Status page. Jean can enter or update the tracking number at any time, independently of the status advancement buttons. The input uses monospace font to make long tracking numbers easier to read and compare.

Print Order Sheet Button

Opens a print-formatted version of the order (Wireframe 16) in a new browser tab, ready to send to the printer. This is the sheet Jean includes in the box with the plants. It shows all plant thumbnails, the customer's name and address, the plant list with quantities, and a thank-you note. Jean specifically requested thumbnails on the order sheet, inspired by Deb's Daylilies.

Confirmed: thumbnails required, non-negotiable
Print Shipping Label Button

Opens a print-formatted shipping label (Wireframe 17) in a new browser tab. Formatted for a standard 4 x 6 inch shipping label. Shows Jean's return address and the customer's shipping address. Jean prints this and affixes it to the box.

Payment Card (sidebar)

Shows payment status (Paid), card type and last four digits from Square, the Square transaction ID for reference, and the total charged. The Square transaction ID is shown in small monospace text since Jean will rarely need it, but it is there if she ever needs to look up a transaction in her Square dashboard. The "Issue refund via Square" link opens Square's dashboard for the relevant transaction.
