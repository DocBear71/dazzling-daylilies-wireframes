Page Annotations: Printable Shipping Label (Wireframe 17 of 17)

Reference notes for WF17. Decisions confirmed during wireframe review are noted below.

Label Dimensions: 4 x 6 Inches

Standard USPS/shipping label size. Jean needs a label printer that supports 4 x 6 labels, or she can print on full-sheet label paper and trim. The most common options are a dedicated thermal label printer (Dymo, Rollo, MUNBYN) or an inkjet/laser printer with Avery-style 4 x 6 label sheets. Jean should confirm which she has or plans to use, since the print setup instructions in her admin guide will differ.

Return Address

The logo appears at the top of the return address section, above the street address. Return address confirmed as: 11247 Scotch Grove Lane, Fairfax, IA 52228. Jean's personal name is not included on the label; she signs the order sheet by hand. The logo serves as the brand identifier.

To Address (main body)

Customer name in large bold text, address in readable but slightly smaller text below. Font sizes are intentionally large: label addresses need to be readable from a distance and by postal workers scanning packages. The city, state abbreviation, and ZIP are on the last line in the standard USPS format.

Order Reference Number

Jean's internal order number (DD-2027-0036) and the order date. This is not a USPS tracking number; it is Jean's own reference so she can match a returned or lost package back to an order in her system. Positioned in the lower section of the label alongside the barcode placeholder.

Barcode Placeholder

The grey barcode illustration is a placeholder only. The actual label does not generate a scannable barcode in this system: Jean uses her own USPS account (Click-N-Ship, Pirateship, or similar) to purchase postage and generate real USPS barcodes. This label is an addressing label, not a postage label. Jean affixes her USPS postage separately.

Live Plants Notice Strip (green, bottom)

A prominent green strip at the bottom of the label indicating the contents are live plants. This signals to postal workers and package handlers to keep the box upright and out of extreme temperatures. It is not a legally required label but it is standard practice for plant shippers and sets appropriate handling expectations.

Variation: Label with Tracking Number Pre-Printed

The second label shown includes the USPS tracking number in a strip above the Live Plants notice. This appears when Jean has already entered a tracking number in the Admin Order Detail page before printing the label. The tracking number is shown in monospace font for readability. Jean can also print the label first (basic version) and enter the tracking number afterwards; both workflows are valid.

Print Setup

Jean confirmed she will use her own inkjet or laser printer with Avery label sheets. Individual labels use Avery 4x6 sheet stock; batch labels use Avery 5163 (2x4 inch, 10 per sheet). Print dialog settings: paper size 4x6 (individual) or 8.5x11 (batch), no scaling (100%), no margins, fit-to-page off. These exact steps will appear in the admin guide with screenshots.
