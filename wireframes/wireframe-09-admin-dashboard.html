Page Annotations: Admin Dashboard (Wireframe 09 of 17)

Reference notes for WF09. Decisions confirmed during wireframe review are noted below.

Admin Top Bar (dark navigation)

Completely separate from the public site navigation. The dark color immediately signals to Jean that she is in the admin area, not browsing as a customer. The six navigation items are: Dashboard, Plants, Orders, Photos, Settings, and Reports. "View Public Site" opens the customer-facing site in a new tab so Jean can check how things look without logging out. "Log Out" ends her session.

Security note: The admin area is protected by Laravel Breeze authentication. Jean must log in with her username and password to access any admin page. If she is not logged in and tries to reach an admin URL, she is redirected to the login page automatically.
Welcome Message and Last Login Time

"Welcome back, Jean" with the current date and her last login time. The last login timestamp is a light security feature: if Jean sees a login time she doesn't recognize, she knows to change her password. Not shown as an alert, just a quiet reassurance.

Stat Cards (four cards across the top)

Four at-a-glance numbers Jean needs every time she opens the admin: orders needing attention (red, because action is required), total orders this season with dollar amount, active plants in the catalog, and plants currently sold out (amber, because it may need attention). Clicking any card navigates to the relevant section. "Orders needing attention" means orders in Received status that Jean has not yet marked as Processing or Shipped.

Quick Action Buttons

Four buttons for the most common tasks: Add New Plant (goes directly to the blank plant editor), View All Orders, Upload Photos, and a red alert button for the 4 orders needing attention. The alert button duplicates the stat card link but is harder to miss for Jean if she is in a hurry. It disappears when there are zero new orders.

Recent Orders Table

Shows the 10 most recent orders. New orders (status: Received, not yet processed) are highlighted in yellow with a red dot indicator. A visual divider separates the new orders from earlier ones. Clicking any row opens the Admin Order Detail page for that order. Columns: order number, date, customer first name and last initial, plant count, total, and status badge.

Business rule: "New" and "Received" are the same status in the database (fulfillment_status = 'received'). The dashboard shows them as "New" with a red dot to make them visually urgent. The order detail page and orders list use the full status labels (Received, Processing, Shipped, Delivered).
Site Visitors Panel (Google Analytics summary)

A compact summary of the last 7 days pulled from Google Analytics 4: total visitors, page views, most-viewed plant, and top referrer source. The mini bar chart shows daily visitor counts with today highlighted in blue. This is a read-only summary; Jean clicks "View full analytics" (not shown in this wireframe) to open the full GA4 dashboard in a new tab.

Sold-Out Plants Panel

Shows the first three sold-out plants with a direct "Edit" link for each, plus a count of additional ones below. Clicking "Edit" opens the Plant Editor for that plant so Jean can restock it (flip the in_stock toggle to true) or hide it (flip is_active to false). This is the fastest path from "I just got more stock" to "the plant is back on the site."

Reminders Panel (yellow card)

A simple list of contextual reminders generated from the current state of the database: unprocessed orders, approaching shipping season end dates, sold-out plant count. These are not configurable by Jean; they generate automatically based on system state. The reminders panel is hidden if there is nothing to remind her of.

Admin Footer

A minimal dark footer matching the top bar, showing who is logged in and quick links to View Public Site and Settings. Kept very simple since Jean will rarely look at the bottom of this page.
