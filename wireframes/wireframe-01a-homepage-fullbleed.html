Homepage (Wireframe 01)

Reference notes for WF01. Decisions confirmed during wireframe review are noted below.

Cookie Notice (dark strip at the very top)

Appears once when a customer first visits. It tells them the site only uses cookies to make the shopping cart work, nothing else. They click "Got it" and it disappears permanently. This is just basic legal housekeeping for a US-only site. No decision needed here.

Option A: Full-Bleed Header — Photo Behind Everything

In this version, the hero photo extends all the way to the very top of the page. "Dazzling Daylilies" and the navigation bar float directly over the flowers, with a dark gradient at the top of the photo to keep the text readable regardless of what the photo looks like. This creates a dramatic, modern look where the first thing customers see is your flowers from edge to edge.

The gradient fades from dark at the very top (behind the site name) to fully transparent in the middle of the photo, so the flowers still show beautifully. The frosted text box with your introduction sits in the center as before.

Option A — please compare with Option B and let Edward know which you prefer
Navigation: Tab Bar (showing launch state)

The tab bar now shows exactly what customers will see on launch day: Daylilies, Collections, and the Cart link. Iris, Lilies, Dahlias, Japanese Iris, and Other Perennials are turned off completely and do not appear at all until you activate them from your admin Settings page. When you are ready to add Iris next year, you flip a switch and it appears. No code changes needed. The wireframe has been updated to show this launch state rather than all possible tabs.

The site is also designed to optionally show a "Coming Soon" label on a tab a few months before you open it for sales, so customers know something new is on the way.

Updated — wireframe now shows actual launch state
Hero Section (large banner photo with frosted text overlay)

A big welcoming photo fills this area. A semi-transparent frosted white box sits over it with a short introduction and a button taking customers to the catalog. The box is see-through enough that the flowers in the background photo are still visible behind the text, as Jean requested. The frosted glass effect keeps text readable while still showing the beauty of the photo.

Confirmed — frosted semi-transparent overlay per Jean's preference Content needed — hero photo still needed from Jean
Featured Plants (4 cards)

Shows four plants from your catalog in this section. You control how they are chosen from a simple dropdown in your admin settings. Three options are available:

  • Featured (hand-picked): You check a box on individual plants in your plant editor and those exact plants appear here. Full control, change them any time.
  • Newest Additions: The site automatically shows your 4 most recently added plants. Good for keeping the homepage feeling fresh as you add inventory.
  • Random: The site picks 4 active plants at random each time the page loads. A nice way to give every plant in your catalog a chance to be seen.

Footer: Three Columns

Left: contact info (email address shown; street address omitted unless you want it public). Middle: links to Shipping Information, Order Status, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service. Right: email newsletter signup.

Footer: Email Signup

Customers type their email and click Subscribe. The contract specifies a Mailchimp signup form. Email addresses collected here go into your Mailchimp list so you can send seasonal updates, new variety announcements, and shipping window reminders. Customers only receive emails if they sign up here; nothing is sent without their permission.

Page Width

Content is contained in a 1,160-pixel-wide column centered on screen, matching the width PlantFans uses. On wide monitors there will be some white space on the sides. On phones everything reflows to a single column with no sideways scrolling.
