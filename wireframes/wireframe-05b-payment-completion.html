Wireframe Notes: WF05B — Payment Completion Page

This page is only reached by customers who placed a "Request Order" from a flagged state (AZ, FL, TX) and received a payment link from Jean by email after she confirmed the shipping cost.

How the Customer Reaches This Page

Jean enters the confirmed shipping amount in the admin order detail page and clicks "Send Payment Link." The system emails the customer a secure, unique link. The customer clicks the link and lands here. The link is tied to their specific order and cannot be reused or shared. It expires after a reasonable time window (to be determined during build, likely 7 days).

Confirmed — Option A flow selected by Jean June 2026
Section 1: Your Information (read-only)

The customer's name, email, and shipping address are pre-filled and read-only. They cannot be changed on this page. If anything is incorrect, the customer is directed to email Jean directly before completing payment. This prevents confusion from the customer re-entering a different shipping address after the shipping cost has already been confirmed.

Read-only fields prevent mismatch between confirmed address and payment
Section 2: Payment

The Square Web Payments SDK card form is embedded here, identical to the standard checkout page. The customer enters their card details and clicks "Complete Payment." The confirmed total is shown clearly both in the order summary and in the button note so there are no surprises. After payment, the order updates to payment_status = paid and the customer is redirected to the standard order confirmation page (WF06).

Same Square integration as standard checkout
Order Summary: Confirmed Shipping

The shipping line shows the amount Jean confirmed, not the original TBD placeholder. A green "✓ Shipping confirmed by Jean" note appears below the total so the customer knows this is the final, agreed amount. This is the only place in the customer flow where the confirmed shipping amount appears before payment.

Shipping amount comes from what Jean entered in the admin panel
What Happens After Payment

On successful payment: order payment_status updates to "paid," the cart session is cleared, and the customer is redirected to the standard WF06 order confirmation page. Jean receives the standard admin order notification email. The order then proceeds through the normal fulfillment workflow: Jean digs, packs, ships, and emails tracking personally.

After payment, flow rejoins the standard order flow
