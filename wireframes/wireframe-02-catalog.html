Wireframe Notes: WF02 — Catalog Page

Hi Jean! The notes below explain what each part of this page does and confirm the decisions we have already made together. When you have finished reading, please use the Approval Form at the bottom of this page to let Edward know whether the page looks right or whether you would like any changes.

Quick Add to Cart button (updated per Jean's request)

Each active plant card now shows both a "View Details" button and a green "+ Cart" button. Clicking "+ Cart" adds one of that plant directly to the cart without needing to visit the detail page first. Sold-out plants still show "Currently Unavailable." Held for Increase plants show "Not Available" with no cart option.

Updated — Jean requested June 2026
Held for Increase badge (purple, matches Sold Out badge position)

Plants Jean is holding for increase show a purple "Held for Increase" badge in the top-right corner of the photo, matching the position and style of the gray "Sold Out" badge. The price line is replaced with a note explaining the plant is not yet available. No cart button appears on these cards.

Updated — Jean requested June 2026
Plant name and attribute line font size (increased per Jean's request)

The plant name is now slightly larger and the attribute line (ploidy, bloom time, scape height) is more readable. The description text below is unchanged.

Updated — Jean requested June 2026
Tab navigation (updated to show launch state)

The tab bar now shows only the tabs that will be active at launch: Daylilies and Collections. Iris, Lilies, Dahlias, Japanese Iris, and Other Perennials are turned off in admin Settings and will not appear at all until Jean activates them. This is how the site will look on day one.

Updated — reflects actual launch state
