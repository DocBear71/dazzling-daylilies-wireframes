Flow Diagram Annotations: Admin Order Processing

Notes explaining Jean's workflow and the key system behaviors at each step.

Starting Trigger: Admin Notification Email

The flow starts when a customer places an order. The system automatically sends Jean a notification email. Jean does not need to be logged into the admin to receive this; it arrives in her inbox like any other email. She then logs in at her convenience to process the order.

Business rule: The admin notification email is sent automatically by the system when an order is placed. Jean does not configure or trigger it manually.
Restricted State Branch (amber path)

If the order has a flagged state indicator (TX, AZ, FL), Jean sees the amber banner in the Order Detail view. Before doing anything else, she should contact the customer by email to confirm the order can be fulfilled. If the customer confirms, Jean continues with the normal workflow. If not, she processes a refund through Square. This is Jean's personal responsibility; the system does not block or automate this check.

Print Order Sheet (WF16)

Jean prints the order sheet first, before packing. The sheet includes plant thumbnails, quantities, the customer's shipping address, and Jean's personal thank-you note. Jean uses it as her packing checklist and places it in the box with the plants. The plant thumbnails are the key feature Jean specifically requested, allowing her to verify visually that she has the right plants without reading every name.

Print Shipping Label (WF17)

Jean prints the shipping label second. This is an address label, not a postage label. Jean affixes her USPS postage separately using her normal method (Click-N-Ship, Pirateship, etc.). The label includes the "Live Plants" notice at the bottom to alert postal workers.

Packing and Shipping (physical steps)

These are physical actions Jean takes in her garden and at the post office. The system has no visibility into this step. Jean digs the bare-root divisions, packs them with the order sheet and care instructions, affixes the label and postage, and drops off the package. This is where Jean's expertise as a grower is most important.

No Automatic Shipping Email (critical)

When Jean clicks "Mark as Shipped," the system updates the order status and records the tracking number. It does NOT send any email to the customer. This is a deliberate design decision matching Jean's stated preference to contact customers personally. The tracking number becomes visible on the customer's Order Status page, which is how customers can check without waiting for Jean's email.

Business rule: No automatic email is sent when an order is marked Shipped. Jean emails customers personally with tracking information and care notes. This is non-negotiable per Jean's workflow preference.
Jean's Personal Email to Customer

Jean sends a personal email from her own email client (not from the admin system) with the tracking number and any plant-specific care advice. This personal touch is part of Jean's brand and customer experience. The admin system supports this workflow by making the tracking number and customer email address easy to find and copy from the Order Detail page.

Batch Processing Loop

During the shipping season Jean may have 10 or more orders to process in a single session. The Prev/Next navigation buttons in the Order Detail view (WF14) let her step through orders without returning to the list between each one. The "More orders to process?" decision at the bottom of the diagram represents this loop. The loop ends when all new orders are processed and the New badge on the Orders list reads zero.

Refund Path (optional, bottom of diagram)

If a refund is needed for any reason (damaged plants, customer cancellation, restricted state complication), Jean processes the refund through Square directly. She then marks the order as Refunded in the admin using the "Mark as Refunded" status update. This is a recommended addition flagged in Wireframe 14 that does not exist in the current wireframe set but should be built. Without it, refunded orders permanently show as "Paid" in Jean's order list.

Flagged decision: Add a "Mark as Refunded" button to the Order Detail page that updates the payment_status field without processing any money movement. Low development cost, meaningful benefit for Jean's end-of-season record-keeping.
