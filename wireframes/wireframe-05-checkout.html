Flagged State Flow (AZ, FL, TX): Option A — Request Order, Pay Later

When a customer in AZ, FL, or TX reaches checkout, they see the agricultural notice and click "I understand." The payment section shows a yellow notice explaining no charge is taken now. The button reads "Request Order" (in orange, not the standard blue Place Order). No card entry form is shown. When they submit, the order is saved with payment_status = pending and fulfillment_status = received. Jean gets a notification, contacts the customer, confirms shipping, enters the amount in admin, and sends a payment link. The customer pays once for the confirmed total via WF05b (Payment Completion page).
