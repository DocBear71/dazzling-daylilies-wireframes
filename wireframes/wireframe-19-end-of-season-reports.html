How This Page Works

The Reports page lives in your admin area, accessible from the left-hand menu. You choose a date range at the top, click Apply, and the two reports update to show only orders within that window. You can then switch between Report 1 (Sales by Category) and Report 2 (Sales by Customer) using the tabs. The Export to Excel button downloads a spreadsheet of whichever report tab you are currently viewing.

Date Range Filter: Confirmed by Jean

You can enter any start and end date to filter the reports. The preset buttons (Full Season, Spring, Fall, Custom) let you jump to common date ranges without typing. Full Season covers the full calendar year. Spring and Fall will match Jean's shipping season windows as configured in Settings. Custom lets you pick any dates you like.

Confirmed: Jean requested date range filtering in her June 5, 2026 reply
Export to Excel: Confirmed by Jean

The Export to Excel button downloads an .xlsx file of the currently visible report. The spreadsheet includes all the same columns shown on screen, with formulas for subtotals and the grand total so you can do your own calculations. This works for both Report 1 and Report 2. The filename will include the report name and date range (for example, "SalesByCategory-2027.xlsx").

Confirmed: Jean requested Excel export in her June 5, 2026 reply
Report 1: Sales by Category

This report lists every plant that sold during the selected period, grouped under its category heading. Each category shows the plant name, quantity sold, unit price, and line total. Below each category's plant list is a subtotal line showing the category quantity and dollar total. Below all the categories is the color-coded summary box Jean requested.

Color-Coded Summary Box: Confirmed by Jean

At the bottom of Report 1, a summary box shows all six plant categories side by side with their season quantity and dollar total. Each category row has a soft background color matching Jean's requested colors: yellow for Daylilies, blue for Iris, green for Lilies, pink for Dahlias, purple for Japanese Iris, and orange for Other Perennials. The colors shown here are soft pastel versions. Categories with zero sales still appear in the box so the full picture is always visible. The grand total appears below the category rows without a color background.

Confirmed: Jean requested this summary box with these category colors in her June 5, 2026 reply
Report 2: Sales by Customer

This report groups sales by customer. Each customer block shows the customer's name, email, and city, followed by a table of their orders during the selected period. Each order row shows the order number (which links back to that order's detail page), the order date, a list of plants ordered, and the order total. The customer's season total appears in the header of their block. A grand total bar at the bottom shows the season total across all customers.

Confirmed: Jean approved this report format in her June 5, 2026 reply
Season Definition

A "season" in these reports is a calendar year (January 1 through December 31). The default date range when you open the page will be the current calendar year. You can change this at any time using the date range filter.

Confirmed: calendar year as season definition
Only Completed Orders Counted

Only orders with a payment status of "Paid" are included in these reports. Cancelled orders, failed payments, and refunded orders are excluded from all totals. This keeps the numbers clean and accurate.

Business rule: reports exclude non-paid orders
