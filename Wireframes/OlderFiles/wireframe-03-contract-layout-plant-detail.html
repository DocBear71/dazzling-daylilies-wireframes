Rich orange and gold blend with a deep, warm throat. Wide, ruffled petals and strong branching make this one of the showiest rebloomers in the catalog. Reliable and easy to grow in most climates.

About This Plant

Acapulco Sunset is one of those varieties that earns its place in every garden. The blooms open a rich orange with gold edges that deepen as the day goes on, giving it a sunset-like quality that photographs beautifully. The scapes are well-branched with good bud count, and it comes back reliably each season even after Iowa winters.


Plants are shipped as bare-root divisions in late spring when conditions are right for safe transit. Each division is dug fresh and packed with care.

Page Annotations: Plant Detail Page — Contract Layout (Wireframe 03 Contract Version)

This version implements the layout specified in the signed contract (Section 3.1): photos on top, all plant information below. What Changed from the Original Wireframe 03

The original Wireframe 03 used a two-column layout: photos on the left half of the screen, plant info on the right half. This version stacks them vertically: photos span the full width at the top, plant information runs below in its own full-width section. The contract (Section 3.1) specifies the stacked layout.

Per signed contract Section 3.1
Photo Layout: Primary + Two Thumbnails

The large primary photo takes up roughly two-thirds of the horizontal space. The two additional photos sit stacked to its right as thumbnails. Clicking a thumbnail swaps it into the large primary position. If a plant has only one photo, the thumbnail column is hidden and the primary photo spans the full width. If a plant has no photos yet, a grey placeholder is shown.

Decision needed: Jean should compare this stacked layout with the two-column layout in the original Wireframe 03 and confirm which she prefers. The contract specifies this version, but the original may feel more natural from her experience with other nursery websites like PlantFans.
Plant Info Section: Attributes Left, Descriptions Right

Below the photos, the info section itself uses a two-column sub-layout: the attribute table (ploidy, bloom size, foliage, etc.) sits on the left, and the short and long descriptions sit on the right. This prevents the attribute table from creating a very long thin column on wide screens. The Add to Cart button spans below both columns.

Six Navigation Tabs

This wireframe also shows all six plant-type tabs from the contract: Daylilies, Iris, Lilies, Dahlias, Japanese Iris, and Other Perennials. Only Daylilies is active at launch. The other five are visible but inactive until Jean activates them from the Settings page as she builds inventory in those categories. The Bundles tab is also shown.

Per signed contract Section 3.1: six tabs including Dahlias and Japanese Iris
Page Length

The stacked layout makes this page taller than the two-column version because all information is sequential rather than parallel. On a typical desktop screen a customer will need to scroll slightly to see the Add to Cart button after reading the descriptions. This is normal and expected for a product detail page of this depth. The photos are immediately visible without scrolling.

Coordinated Flowers and Bundle Panel (unchanged)

The Coordinated Flowers section below the plant info is identical to the revised Wireframe 03. The Bundle offer panel, editorial browse grid, and all bundle functionality are unchanged regardless of which photo layout Jean chooses.
