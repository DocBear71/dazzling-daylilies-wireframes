Page Annotations: Plant Detail Page — Revised (Wireframe 03)

This revision adds the Bundle Offer Panel above the existing Coordinated Flowers editorial grid. The upper product section (photos, attributes, Add to Cart) is unchanged from the original Wireframe 03.

What Changed from Original Wireframe 03

One new panel was inserted between the main product section and the existing editorial Coordinated Flowers grid. Everything else on the page is identical to the original wireframe. The editorial grid now has a heading ("Or browse plants individually") to clarify its relationship to the bundle offer above it.

New element
Bundle Offer Panel: Layout

Green-bordered panel to visually separate it from the grey product section above. Left side shows a "Bundle & Save" label, the bundle name (e.g., "Jean's Sunset Garden Collection"), chip-style plant thumbnails for every plant in the set, and the pricing block. Right side shows the plant count and the primary call-to-action button ("View Bundle Details") which navigates to Wireframe 03b. The bundle panel only appears on this page if Jean has defined a bundle and set a bundle price for this plant's coordinated set. If no bundle is configured, the section shows only the editorial grid.

New element
Bundle Name

"Jean's Sunset Garden Collection" is placeholder copy showing that Jean can give each bundle a descriptive name. This name is set in the Admin Plant Editor (Wireframe 11, revised). If Jean does not enter a name, a default like "5-Plant Collection" is used. The name helps customers understand what they are buying as a set.

Decision needed: Should bundle names be required or optional? A meaningful name (like Jean's example above) helps customers but adds a step for Jean when configuring bundles. Optional with a sensible default is the recommendation.
Pricing Display: Bundle Price, Individual Total, Savings

Three elements shown together: the bundle price ($112.00), the crossed-out individual total ($120.00), and a savings badge ("Save $8.00"). The individual total is calculated automatically by summing the current prices of all plants in the set. This means if Jean updates a plant's price, the individual total and savings badge update automatically on every bundle that includes that plant.

Jean-flagged: Should the discount be a fixed dollar amount Jean sets, or a percentage she defines? The wireframe shows a fixed dollar amount (bundle price vs. sum of individual prices). Jean decides.
"View Bundle Details" Button

The primary call to action on the bundle panel. Navigates to Wireframe 03b, the dedicated bundle page, where the customer sees the full plant list, individual photos, and a single "Add Bundle to Cart" button. The customer does not add the bundle directly from this panel; they go to the bundle page first. This gives them a chance to see the full set before committing.

Decision needed: Should there also be an "Add Bundle to Cart" button directly on this panel (skipping the bundle detail page), or is the two-step flow (panel then bundle page) preferable? Direct add is faster; the intermediate page gives more context and reduces buyer hesitation on a larger purchase.
Bundle Panel Visibility Logic

The bundle panel only renders if Jean has configured a bundle for this plant (i.e., the plant_coordinations record has a non-null bundle_price_cents value). If no bundle is configured, the Coordinated Flowers section shows only the editorial grid, exactly as in the original Wireframe 03. Jean controls this entirely from the Admin Plant Editor.

Existing Editorial Grid (unchanged)

The individual plant cards below the bundle panel are identical to the original wireframe. The new heading "Or browse plants individually" makes the relationship clear: customers can either take the bundle deal or pick plants one at a time. Both options are always visible when a bundle exists.
