Rich orange and gold blend with a deep, warm throat. Wide, ruffled petals and strong branching make this one of the showiest rebloomers in the catalog. Reliable and easy to grow in most climates.

About This Plant

Acapulco Sunset is one of those varieties that earns its place in every garden. The blooms open a rich orange with gold edges that deepen as the day goes on, giving it a sunset-like quality that photographs beautifully. The scapes are well-branched with good bud count, and it comes back reliably each season even after Iowa winters.


Plants are shipped as bare-root divisions in late spring when conditions are right for safe transit. Each division is dug fresh and packed with care.
