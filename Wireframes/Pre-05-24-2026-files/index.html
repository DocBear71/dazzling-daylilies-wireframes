Plant Detail Page — Four Versions to Compare
Please review all four versions below and tell Edward which you prefer. The main question is layout: photos on the LEFT with info on the RIGHT (side-by-side), or photos on TOP with info BELOW (stacked). Your signed contract specifies the stacked layout, but Edward wants you to see both before the build begins. The bundle feature (panel and separate catalog page) is new and also needs your input. Scroll to the bottom of each wireframe to answer the questions.
