Page Annotations and Your Feedback

Hi Jean! This is the form you will use every time you add or edit a plant in your catalog. It has a lot of fields, but most have sensible defaults and you only fill in what you know. The annotations above explain every section. The questions below need your input before Edward begins building. Click Send My Feedback when done.

Admin Plant Editor (WF11 — Original)

This is the original plant editor without the bundle configuration fields. For the version with bundle setup, see Wireframe 11 (Revised). Answer the questions below about the fields and workflow shown here.

Unsaved Changes Warning

The sticky save bar at the top warns you when you have unsaved changes. There is also an option to show a browser warning if you try to leave the page with unsaved changes.

Plant Type Dropdown

The six plant types are: Daylily, Iris, Lily, Dahlia, Japanese Iris, Other Perennial. These match the six navigation tabs on the public site.

URL Slug

The slug is generated automatically from the plant name (Acapulco Sunset becomes acapulco-sunset). It is used in the web address for that plant's page and as the photo filename prefix. Changing it after a plant is live can break things.

Attribute Dropdowns

Color, Bloom Size, Ploidy, Foliage, Bloom Time, Rebloomer, and Fragrant are all dropdown fields with fixed option lists. If you need a new value added (a new color category, for example), Edward would need to make that change for you.

Short Description vs. Long Description

Short Description appears on the catalog card and at the top of the detail page. Long Description appears only on the detail page below the attributes. Both are optional. Internal Notes are private and never shown to customers.

Coordinated With (companion plant selector)

You type a plant name in the search box and select companions from your own catalog. They appear as chips you can remove. The relationship is one-directional: you have to set both directions manually if you want a pairing to appear on both plants' detail pages.

Photo Management (sidebar)

Up to 3 photos per plant. The first photo is the primary (shown in catalog cards, order sheets, and emails). You can drag to reorder them. Clicking the plus button lets you upload photos for this specific plant, as an alternative to the bulk uploader.

Active and In Stock Toggles (sidebar)

Active controls whether the plant is visible on your public site at all. In Stock controls whether it can be added to a cart. These are independent: you can have a plant be active but sold out (visible with a sold-out badge), or inactive and in-stock (hidden from customers but ready to go live).

Delete Plant (bottom of page)

Deleting a plant hides it from your public catalog immediately but does not permanently erase it. Edward can recover a deleted plant within 30 days. A confirmation dialog appears before anything is deleted. Past orders that included the plant are not affected.
