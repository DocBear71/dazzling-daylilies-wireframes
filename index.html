New Features Added Since Your Last Review
Based on your feedback, the following features have been added to the build plan:
  • Local Pickup: Customers can pay online and pick up at your house. No shipping charge for pickup orders.
  • Held for Increase Status: List a plant so customers can see it exists, without being able to order it, while it grows out.
  • Notify Me When Available: Customers on sold-out plant pages can request an email notification when it is back in stock.
  • Quantity Tracking: Stock counts on all plants. System automatically marks a plant sold out when the last one is purchased.
  • Preferred Ship Date: Customers can enter a preferred shipping date at checkout. Shown on your order detail and printed order sheet.
  • Coupon Codes: You create and manage coupon codes from the admin panel. Customers enter them at checkout.
  • Shipping Season Banner: A banner appears on your site automatically when a shipping window is active, showing what is currently shipping and the dates.
  • FAQ Section on Shipping Page: You add, edit, and reorder frequently asked questions from your admin panel. They appear automatically on the Shipping Information page.
  • End of Season Reports: Sales by plant category with totals, and sales by customer. Details being confirmed with Jean.
  • Batch Shipping Labels: Select multiple orders and print all of their shipping labels on one Avery 5163 sheet.
  • Printable Inventory Report: A printable hard copy of your full inventory by plant category, sorted alphabetically. (WF18, coming soon.)
  • Flagged State Payment Flow (Option A): Customers in AZ, FL, and TX click "Request Order" at checkout. No charge is made. Jean contacts them, confirms shipping, and sends a secure payment link. Customer pays once for the confirmed total. New Payment Completion page (WF05B) added.
  • Carrier-Neutral Tracking: Jean selects the shipping carrier (USPS, UPS, FedEx, or Other) when marking an order shipped. The customer's Order Status page shows a direct tracking link to the correct carrier automatically.
