Dazzling Daylilies — Wireframe Review

This page contains all wireframes, flow diagrams, and the design decisions document for Jean's review. Click any link to open that wireframe in your browser. Each wireframe includes an annotations section at the bottom explaining what each part of the page does.

Client: Jean — Dazzling Daylilies Developer: Edward G. McKeown Status: Awaiting Jean's review and feedback
Hi Jean! Each link below opens a wireframe page showing what that part of your website will look like. These are deliberately simple grey-and-white sketches, not the final design — colors, photos, and fonts come later. Scroll to the bottom of each page to read the annotations, which explain in plain English what each section does and flag any questions that need your input. When you are done reviewing, send Edward your feedback on each page.
Customer-Facing Pages
WF 01
Homepage
The first page customers see at dazzlingdaylilies.com. Hero section, featured plants, about Jean, footer.
 WF 02
Catalog Page
The main daylily browsing page. Filter sidebar, sort options, plant cards, pagination.
 WF 03
Plant Detail Page Revised
Individual plant page. Photos, attributes, Add to Cart, and the new Bundle offer panel.
 WF 03b
Bundle Detail Page New
The dedicated page for a single plant bundle. Full plant list with photos, pricing, and Add Bundle to Cart.
 WF 03c
Bundles Catalog Page New
Standalone page listing all bundles Jean has configured. Browsable independently at /bundles.
 WF 04
Cart Page
Shopping cart with line items, shipping calculation, and Proceed to Checkout. Includes empty cart state.
 WF 05
Checkout Page
Customer info, shipping address, state restriction notices, Square payment, Place Order. Three states shown.
 WF 06
Order Confirmation
Thank you page after a successful order. Order number, plant list, Jean's personal note, next steps.
 WF 07
Order Status Page
Lookup form plus order detail view. Three states: lookup form, Processing, and Shipped with tracking number.
 WF 08
Shipping Information Page
Public shipping policy. Rates, season dates, state restrictions, care instructions on arrival.

 WF 11

 WF 12

Shipping rates, season dates, restricted states, plant tabs, shipping policy text, Mailchimp, account.
